FOUR STEPS

From first conversation
to launch day.

We keep the process collaborative and transparent, so you know what we are building and why each step matters.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn about your business, audience, services and goals.

    What happens
    • We ask about your business, your services and your customers
    • We learn what you want the website to achieve
    • We agree what the website needs to include
    What we need from you: A description of your business and services, and any material you already have, such as a logo or photos.
  2. 02

    Plan & Design

    We organise your content and create a visual direction for the website.

    What happens
    • Your content is organised into clear pages and sections
    • A visual direction is created for your website
    • You review the direction and share your feedback
    What we need from you: Your feedback on the direction, and the text and images you want included.
  3. 03

    Build

    We develop and test your website across different screen sizes.

    What happens
    • Your pages are developed and your content is added
    • The website is tested on phones, tablets and desktop screens
    • Buttons, links and contact options are checked
    What we need from you: Quick answers to any questions that come up while we build.
  4. 04

    Launch

    After your review and approval, we prepare the website for launch.

    What happens
    • You review the finished website
    • Once you approve it, we prepare it for launch
    • We talk through how to keep it updated and supported
    What we need from you: Your final review and approval.
QUESTIONS

Questions we
often hear.

Can't find your answer here? Message us and we will help.

What do I need to get started?

Your business name, what you offer and what you want the website to achieve. If you already have a logo, photos or written content, bring them along. If you do not, we will discuss what is needed.

How much will my website cost?

The cost depends on the type of website, the number of pages and the features you need. Tell us about your project and we will discuss it with you.

How long will it take?

It depends on the size of the website and how quickly content and feedback come in. We will talk through timing once we understand your project.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. We design and test every website across different screen sizes, including phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. We offer website redesign and upgrades, as well as maintenance and technical support.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. See our e-commerce websites and online stores, or tell us what you want to sell and we will discuss the best approach.
